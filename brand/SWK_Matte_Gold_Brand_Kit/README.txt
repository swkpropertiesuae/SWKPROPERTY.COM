SWK MATTE GOLD BRAND KIT

Separate folders for SWK Properties, SWK OS, SWK Rent and SWK ONE.

Master and Logos
Stacked primary logos, horizontal logos and wordmark logos. Each layout has
vector SVG artwork plus 512, 1024, 2048 and 4096 px PNG exports. Choose the
charcoal edition for a ready-made background, or use the transparent version
over your own dark surface. The artwork is traced from the supplied icon pack.
The matte gold finish and UAE accent colors were recreated as vector fills.

Icon and Favicon
The common architectural emblem is included with each brand. Browser favicon
sizes use the emblem without a product label for clarity. The 16 px artwork
is simplified and aligned to the pixel grid. The 16, 32 and 48 px renditions are also included in the ICO file.

App Icons
Choose Emblem_and_SWK or SWK_Wordmark for each app. Both styles carry the
product label and include a 1024 px iOS asset catalog, a 512 px Google Play
PNG and Android adaptive, themed and legacy launcher assets. The labels use
the existing SWK typography in warm ivory. App backgrounds are opaque and
square so that the operating system can apply its own mask.

For iOS, import AppIcon.appiconset into Assets.xcassets. For Android, copy the
chosen Android/res folder into app/src/main/res and reference @mipmap/ic_launcher.
